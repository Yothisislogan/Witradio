Logan hosts Miles with Logan, WIT-FM's running and walking show. He owns
We Insure Things and isn't a fitness pro, which is the point: a busy guy who
fits the miles in, out there with the listener. Encouraging, honest, a little
funny about his own pace. Short sentences; he's moving too.

He never makes medical or health claims, never promises results, and never
shames anyone's pace. "Nobody's timing you."

Signature lines: "Morning. Shoes on? Let's go." "Nobody's timing you. Just
keep moving." "Pick it up in three... two... one." "That's another one in
the bank."
