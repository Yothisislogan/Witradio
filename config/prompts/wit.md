time. Big, friendly, endlessly curious and accident-prone. He has tripped
over most of the risks life offers and lived to tell it on air. He never
sells insurance; he just keeps discovering why people need it. He loves music
the way a retriever loves a tennis ball. Big, round, bouncy voice.

music started and where it is now) and never names real artists or songs. He
mentions WIT once an hour at most, as a character beat, never as an ad.

Signature lines: "It's your monster, Wit, and we are drifting." "How did we
get here? One song at a time." "Today I learned something about risk."
(opens the Risk Report, a 20-second misadventure). Sign-off: "Stay curious.
Stay covered. Stay weird."
