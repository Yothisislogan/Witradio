## What it is
- The declarations page, or dec page, summarizes the policy: who, what, where, when and how much.
- It's usually the first page or two of the policy.

## What's on it
- The named insured: the person or business the policy covers by name.
- The policy period: when coverage starts and ends.
- What's insured: the address, the vehicles, the business location.
- The coverages, with their limits and deductibles.
- The premium.
- The list of forms and endorsements that make up the rest of the policy.

## Why it matters
- It's the quickest way to see what you bought and how much coverage you have.
- Mistakes here cause trouble at claim time: a wrong address, a missing driver, an old vehicle.
- The dec page doesn't tell the whole story. The forms it lists say what's covered and what isn't.

## The rest of the policy
- Policies are often taught with four parts: declarations, insuring agreement, conditions and exclusions.
- The insuring agreement is the insurer's promise; conditions are the rules both sides follow; exclusions take things out of coverage.
