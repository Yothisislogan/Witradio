# War Stories

One story per file, as `<id>.md`, with front matter:

```markdown
---
title: "The slow leak behind the dishwasher"
source: wit_claim        # wit_claim, court_case or catastrophe_report
citation: ""             # for a court case or a public report: where it's published
lesson: "Gradual damage is commonly excluded; sudden and accidental damage is commonly covered."
reviewed_by: null
reviewed_on: null
---
- One fact per line, in the order the story goes.
```

The show bible's rule: "No personal information in War Stories, ever." A
story from WIT's own claims has every name, address, policy number and
identifying detail stripped before it's written down, and it belongs in
WRIT_OFFICE_HOURS_DIR (default ~/.writ/office_hours/war_stories/), not in
this public repository. The producer also refuses any story whose notes look
like they hold an email address, phone number, street address or policy
number.

No stories ship here: they have to be real, and they have to be yours.
