# WIT Radio schedule

All times use the broadcast server’s local timezone. The canonical schedule is `config/schedule.yaml`; host identities are in `config/wit_dj_personas.yaml`.

| Time | Show | Host | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00:00–06:00 | The Night Shift | Nyx | Late-night lo-fi, synthwave, ambient electronic atmosphere. |
| 06:00–09:00 | Morning Acoustic | Sunny | Coffeehouse acoustic, folk-pop, peaceful sunrise energy. |
| 09:00–20:00 | The Day Shift | Dash | Upbeat workday music, modern pop, indie, positive energy. |
| 20:00–00:00 | Dance Party | Nova | EDM, house, dance-pop, club energy, Brazilian phonk. |

Each show schedules brief `dj_break` segments between music blocks. WIT links target 15–30 seconds with a 35-second rendered ceiling and at most four per rolling hour.
